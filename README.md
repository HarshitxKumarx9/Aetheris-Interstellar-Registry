# Aetheris-Interstellar-Registry
AIR: The Aetheris Interstellar Registry is a data-driven ledger of the 2026 Strategic Shortlist . Synthesizing 37 scientific sources, it profiles 45 actionable rocky worlds—including LHS 1140 b and TRAPPIST-1 i—to guide the mission for life . A minimalist record of the shift from planet discovery to targeted strategy.
