Overview
The Aetheris Interstellar Registry (AIR) is a high-density, data-driven "Master Ledger" documenting the fundamental shift in astrobiology from survey-driven discovery to the strategic prioritization of habitable environments
. Grounded in the 2026 Bohl-Kaltenegger Catalogue, this project identifies and profiles the 45 rocky exoplanets most actionable for atmospheric characterization by next-generation facilities like the Habitable Worlds Observatory (HWO) and PLATO
.
Key Registry Modules
This repository hosts a single-page web interface spanning 30+ data modules, designed for extensive vertical scrolling and high-fidelity technical review.
1. The 2026 Strategic Shortlist
The registry prioritizes 45 rocky exoplanets located in the empirical Habitable Zone (HZ) and 24 worlds in the narrower, conservative 3D Habitable Zone
. These targets are filtered to maximize the scientific yield for reflected-light spectroscopy missions
.

TRAPPIST-1 i (The 8th Neighbor): Documentation of the eighth terrestrial world in the system, detected via Transit Timing Variations (TTV)
. It possesses a Mars-like mass and is locked in a stable 3:2 Laplace resonance with planets g and h
.

3. Biosignature Lab
The DMS Signal: Independent 3-sigma evidence for Dimethyl Sulfide (DMS) and DMDS in the hydrogen-rich atmosphere of the Hycean world K2-18 b
.
NASA Science Gaps: Comprehensive listing of the 17 critical science gaps (SCI-01 to SCI-17) identified by NASA’s ExEP to refine the search for life
.
4. Mission Architecture & Infrastructure
PLATO (2026): Integration of the PLATO Input Catalog (PIC), featuring 290,000 stars and a 2-billion-pixel survey architecture—the highest pixel count ever flown on a space mission
.
Future Roadmap: Tracking milestones for the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope (2027), the Extremely Large Telescope (2029), and HWO
.
Core Design Principles
Minimalist Aesthetic: A strict black-on-white palette eliminates visual noise to focus on raw scientific data.
Fluid Motion: Vertical "Upward-Reveal" animations utilize the IntersectionObserver API to ensure a smooth, continuous data stream during deep scrolling.
Everyman Explanations: Complex astrophysical concepts like "Mean Motion Resonance" and "Transmission Spectroscopy" are paired with accessible analogies like the "Twin Star Dance" and "Smelling the Air"
